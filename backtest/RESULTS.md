## Tested and not adopted

Relative-momentum version of the trend sleeve (hold the top 3 of the 8 ETFs by 13/26/52-week return, re-ranked every 4 weeks): 8.3% a year, 13.3% volatility, Sharpe 0.53, worst drawdown 28.9%. Higher return for about three times the drawdown and no better risk-adjusted result, so the equal-slice version stays.

## Limits of this test

- SPY is the only instrument with real, dividend-adjusted open prices. The three index series are price indexes with unreliable opens (the Russell 2000 open equals the prior close on 36.7% of days), so they use close fills and stand in for QQQ, IWM and DIA.
- The trend sleeve has 20 years of ETF history, one sample that includes 2008, 2020 and 2022.
- Weekly ETF data is from Alpha Vantage, daily data from Financial Modeling Prep. SPY daily returns were checked against a second source for 1993 to 2019 (correlation 0.9999); the other series were not cross-checked.
- About 75 rule variants were run in total. The defaults were fixed from the published rules before any were run, and are not the best performers.
